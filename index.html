Process

From Concept to Gerber

A verified, fixed-price workflow for professional PCB delivery

01

Brief

Fill out the intake form with your board purpose, layer count, component count, and deadline — takes about 5 minutes.

02

Quote

A clear, itemised fixed quote lands in your inbox within 24 hours. No surprise fees — ever. Pro/Complex tiers require a 50% deposit to start.

03

Schematic

We draw the full schematic in KiCad and send you a PDF for review and sign-off. Routing doesn't begin until you approve it.

04

Layout

Full PCB layout routed to JLCPCB/PCBWay design rules. We run DRC, share the 3D render and Gerber preview for your sign-off before export.

05

Gerbers

We package and export the complete production file set: Gerbers, drill files, BOM, schematic PDF, pick-and-place CSV, and a 3D render.

06

Delivery

Files delivered via a secure link within the hour — or add the $5 fab coordination add-on and we submit directly to JLCPCB or PCBWay on your behalf.
