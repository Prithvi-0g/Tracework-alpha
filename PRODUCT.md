# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineers, students, and hardware makers who have a schematic and need production-ready PCB files without the back-and-forth of hiring a full-time designer or navigating a freelancer marketplace. They know what KiCad is. They know what Gerbers are. They want to hand off a problem and get back files they can send to JLCPCB today.

## Product Purpose

TraceWorks takes a schematic, board constraints, and manufacturer target and returns a complete fabrication package: routed layout, DRC-verified, Gerbers, drill files, BOM, CPL, schematic PDF. Run by Prithvi Gupta (Nashua, NH). Fixed price. Same-day turnaround on standard work. No dashboards, no subscriptions, no vague promises — just files.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Fast. Technical.

The brand speaks to engineers as equals. It does not explain what a PCB is. It does not promise to "revolutionize" anything. It says what it does, shows evidence of doing it, and makes the path to getting files as short as possible.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing page: dark background with purple/violet gradient accent, metric cards, glowing orbs, animated gradient text, hero with "10x faster" copywriting. This is the primary thing to avoid — the current site leans into this and it undercuts technical credibility.
- Fiverr / freelancer marketplace aesthetic: badge-heavy, discount-feeling, crowded, review-star UI.
- Dry legacy PCB manufacturer site: gray corporate, no personality, reads like a PDF.
- AI hype site: glowing orbs, buzzword-heavy, vague capability claims.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Show the boards, the file outputs, the real terminal output — don't claim quality, demonstrate it.
2. **Speak the language.** Every word assumes the reader knows KiCad, Gerbers, and DRC. No onboarding copy for concepts engineers already own.
3. **Studio, not marketplace.** The aesthetic is a sharp independent hardware studio, not a platform or SaaS. Closer to Oxide Computer in credibility, Teenage Engineering in distinctiveness, Linear in precision.
4. **Commit to the material.** PCBs have a visual language — FR4 green, copper traces, gold pads, silkscreen white. That material vocabulary can anchor the visual identity without becoming a costume.
5. **Earn every pixel.** No decorative motion, no padding sections, no filler copy. Each element either sells the service or gets cut.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced-motion support required (the current site has a prefers-reduced-motion block — preserve it). No known specific user accommodations beyond standard contrast and focus management.
